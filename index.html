Dzongkha · Bhutan's national language

Dzongkha carries identity, tradition, governance, literature, and everyday communication. This app helps keep that knowledge searchable and useful.

Importance of Dzongkha

Language is memory in motion.

IdentityDzongkha connects people to Bhutanese history, values, and national belonging.
ServiceClear terminology helps language officers, translators, educators, and readers work with confidence.
ContinuitySearchable language resources make traditional knowledge easier to preserve and share.

App Creator

Jamyang Loday

Jamyang Loday is a Bachelor of Arts graduate from the College of Language and Culture Studies with a specialization in Language and Literature. Presently, he serves as an Assistant Language Development Officer in the Dzongkha Development Division since 2024.

His notable accomplishment includes securing the top position in the RCSE 2023 in the Dzongkha category. In the past, he worked as a Substitute Teacher, gaining valuable experience in education.

Beyond his academic and professional endeavors, Jamyang has skills in graphic design, particularly in designing books, journals, and logos. He also has a keen interest and experience in photography.
