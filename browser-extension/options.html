Quick Lookup settings

Open the dictionary once to connect it automatically, or enter its public web address below.

How to use it

  1. Keep the Dzongkha Dictionary open in a tab or installed app window.
  2. Select an English or Dzongkha word on another webpage.
  3. Press ⌘ C twice on macOS or Ctrl C twice on Windows.

The normal copy action is preserved. Quick Lookup does not monitor text until you copy a selection twice.
